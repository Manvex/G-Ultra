# Contributing

Bug reports and pull requests are welcome.

- **Bugs:** include your Windows or Android version, your device model, and what you expected to happen.
- **Pull requests:** keep them focused on one change, and describe how you tested it.
- **Safety:** cleanup, process and fan code needs extra care. Run the self-test described in the README when you change it.

G Ultra is offered under GPL-3.0 and may also be offered under separate commercial licenses. By submitting a contribution, you agree that it may be distributed under both.
